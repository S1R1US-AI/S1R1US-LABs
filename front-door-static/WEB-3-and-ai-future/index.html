Q: Bull case for AI, bitcoin, and AI trading desks?

A: AI spreads as research and decision support. Bitcoin rails deepen. Trading desks stay paper or future-design, with human locks and audits—not free-running autonomy.

Q: What if everyone runs similar software?

A: The network stays up. Congestion and copycat strategies rise; markets get noisier. Advantage shifts to better data, risk rules, and kill-switches. Homogeneous bots can amplify mistakes.

Q: Does the internet get overloaded, die, or evolve toward Web3?

A: Evolve. Classic web remains; NIST-style Web3 (identity, verifiable data, selective contracts) grows beside it. Capacity and protocol upgrades absorb load—not a single flip to “all Web3.” NIST Web3 ≠ W3C Web 3.0.

Q: Can quantum security protect bitcoin?

A: Over a long horizon, NIST post-quantum crypto (FIPS 203–205 path) is the practical track: migrate signatures and related material through careful community upgrades.
