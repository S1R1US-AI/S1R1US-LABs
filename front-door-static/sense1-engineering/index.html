Sensei (definition)

Sensei (先生) is an honorific used in the Sinosphere. In Japanese it literally means “person born before another” or “one who comes before.” It is generally used after a person’s name and means “teacher.” It is also used as a title for professionals or people of authority, or to show respect to someone who has achieved mastery in an art or skill.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensei
