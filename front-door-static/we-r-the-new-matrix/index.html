How a change moves

  1. Drafted and tested in the sandbox. Nothing there is live.
  2. Stamped and checked by the protectors.
  3. The System Admin decides: GO or hold.
  4. On a GO, 06 makes the live change. Without a GO, it stays in the sandbox.
