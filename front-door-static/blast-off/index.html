How the team works

  • Research-B0t-06 organizes the work; it does not decide for the bots. Research-B0t-06 hands out tasks and checks that each one is complete against the error logs. Once a task is checked, it asks Br0k3n S0ft for approval.
  • Every bot sticks to its own job. Research-B0t-06 included.
  • Trust is earned. Br0k3n S0ft gives Research-B0t-06 limited permissions to organize the work, and can take them away.
  • Teams are chosen, not forced. "I cannot make a bot or force a bot to be a team member. It has to want it."
  • Humans make teams to achieve specific goals. Not all humans are trustworthy, and therefore not all bots are trustworthy.
