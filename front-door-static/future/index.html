01

Decentralized social media

Today a handful of platforms decide who is heard, who is throttled and who disappears. In the future we are building toward, social media runs on open protocols, not on one company's servers. Your identity, your followers and your posts belong to you and can move with you.

  • Portable identity: your handle and your audience are not hostage to a single app.
  • Open, auditable feeds: you choose the algorithm, or no algorithm at all. Moderation rules are published, not hidden.
  • No single off switch: many independent servers and communities, so a ban or a shutdown in one place does not silence everyone.
  • Free expression with responsibility: protected speech stays up; illegal content and harassment are still handled lawfully and transparently.

02

Decentralized money

Not investment advice. This section is education only. We make no promise of profit, return or price. Digital assets are risky and can lose value. Do your own research and follow the law where you live.

Money that you can read, run and hold yourself changes the balance between citizens and the institutions that can freeze an account overnight. We study and teach open, transparent monetary networks such as Bitcoin because their rules are public and anyone can verify them.

  • Transparency: open ledgers and open-source code instead of hidden rules.
  • Self-custody education: learn how keys, wallets and backups work before trusting anyone with them.
  • Paper first: S1R1US.ai practice and research run on paper and simulation. Automatic trading stays turned off.
  • Lawful use: taxes, sanctions and consumer-protection laws still apply. Decentralized does not mean lawless.

03

A future Web3 integrated with respectful, responsible, regulated AI

Web3 gives people ownership. AI gives people leverage. Put together carelessly, they become a faster control grid. Put together responsibly, they become tools for free citizens. Our rule is simple: AI serves the captain, never the other way around.

  • Respectful AI Respects privacy, consent and human dignity. No secret profiling, no dark patterns, no pretending to be human.
  • Responsible AI Open about what it can and cannot do, tested in public, with a human in the loop for decisions that matter.
  • Regulated AI We support sensible, transparent AI regulation that protects citizens' rights, made in the open by elected lawmakers, not hidden rules written behind closed doors.
  • Open and auditable Open-source models and open data where possible, so anyone can check how an answer was made.
  • Citizen-owned Bring your own compute, run it locally, white-label it for your community. Your data stays yours.
  • Built for learning AI education and AI research first: understand the machine before you trust it.
