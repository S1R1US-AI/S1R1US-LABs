DMT and plant medicine FAQ

What is DMT?

DMT (N,N-Dimethyltryptamine) is a naturally occurring tryptamine found in many plants and detected in animals. It is a powerful, short-acting psychedelic that scientists study in labs and clinical trials. Overview: DMT on Wikipedia ↗.

What is ayahuasca?

Ayahuasca is a traditional plant brew from the Amazon basin (Peru, Brazil, Colombia and Ecuador). It is usually made from the Banisteriopsis caapi vine together with leaves that contain DMT, and Indigenous peoples of the Amazon use it in ceremony. Overview: Ayahuasca on Wikipedia ↗.

Is DMT legal?

In the United States, DMT is a Schedule I controlled substance under the Controlled Substances Act (DEA drug code 7435). Laws differ by country. S1R1US.ai does not encourage anyone to use, buy, grow or seek any controlled substance unlawfully.
